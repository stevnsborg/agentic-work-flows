# Document structures

Headings in English with Danish equivalents. Use the association's language in the output.

## Notice of ordinary general meeting [Indkaldelse til ordinær generalforsamling]

```
<Association name>
<Place>, <date sent>

Notice of ordinary general meeting
The board hereby gives notice of the ordinary general meeting
  Date and time: <weekday, date, time>
  Place: <address, room>

Agenda [Dagsorden]
  1. Election of chair of meeting and minute-taker
  2. The board's report for the past year
  3. Presentation and approval of the annual accounts
  4. Presentation and approval of the budget, incl. common charges
  5. Proposals received
     5.1 <title> (proposed by <board / owner, unit>)
  6. Election of board members and alternates
  7. Election of auditor
  8. Any other business (no resolutions can be passed under this item)

Attached: annual accounts, budget, proposals, proxy form
Proxy: <rules from bylaws: who may hold a proxy, how many>
Questions: <contact>

Kind regards
The board
```
The list above is a common default. Replace it with the mandatory agenda from the bylaws.

## Proposal [Forslag]

```
Proposal <no.>: <title>
Proposed by: <board / owner, unit>
Proposal (to be voted on): "<exact wording>"
Background and reasons: <short>
Cost and financing: <amount, from reserves / extra charge / loan; effect per unit or per share>
Majority required: <simple / qualified, bylaws §x>
```

## Board's annual report [Bestyrelsens beretning]

1. The board in the past year (members, meetings held).
2. Building and maintenance (work done, from the work-order register; maintenance plan).
3. Apartment alterations (number approved, any principles decided).
4. Finances (headline figures from the accounts, reserves, common charges).
5. Community and house rules.
6. The coming year (planned work, proposals, challenges).
7. Thanks.

## Approval of apartment alteration [Godkendelse af ombygning]

```
<Owner name>, <unit>
Date: <date>
Re: Your request of <date> regarding <work>

The board approved the work described in your request at its meeting on <date>, subject to the following conditions:
  1. The work is carried out by qualified/authorised tradespeople; wet-room work to current standard, with documentation sent to the board.
  2. Any required building permit is obtained before work starts. [[check if needed]]
  3. Working hours: <weekday hours>. Neighbours are notified at least <n> days before noisy work.
  4. Stairwell and common areas are protected and cleaned; building waste is removed by the owner.
  5. The owner pays all costs, including any damage to common parts, and takes over maintenance of the altered installations.
  6. The board is notified at start and completion. The board may inspect the work.
  7. <specific conditions>

Kind regards
The board
```
Adjust the standard conditions to what the bylaws and earlier board decisions actually say.

## Stairwell notice [Opslag]

Title in large type with the date. Three lines: what happens, what you must do, who to contact. Nothing else.
