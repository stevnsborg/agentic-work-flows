# Glossary

Format: `term — meaning`. Add association-specific nicknames and abbreviations as they come up.

- ejerforening — owners' association
- bestyrelse — board
- formand / næstformand / kasserer — chair / vice chair / treasurer
- generalforsamling (ordinær / ekstraordinær) — general meeting (annual / extraordinary)
- indkaldelse — notice of meeting
- dagsorden — agenda
- referat — minutes
- vedtægter — bylaws / articles
- husorden — house rules
- fællesudgifter — common charges paid by owners
- fordelingstal / ejerandel — allocation key / ownership share used to split costs
- vedligeholdelsesplan — maintenance plan
- grundfond / henlæggelser — reserve fund / provisions for future maintenance
- administrator — managing agent (handles accounts, collections, sometimes contracts)
- ombygning / ændring af lejlighed — apartment alteration (e.g. kitchen, bathroom, walls)
- byggetilladelse — building permit
- restance — arrears
